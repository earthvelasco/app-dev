## Overview
*Stranger Things* is a sci-fi horror series set in the 1980s.  
It focuses on a group of kids who uncover supernatural mysteries in their small town.

### Main Characters
- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair

> “Friends don’t lie.” — Eleven

**Why I love it:**  
The mix of nostalgia, mystery, and friendship makes it unforgettable!
